Mode, mean, Median, Standard Deviation, Variance calculator also finds the shape of the distribution. The more will be added(I think).

I inspired to do this from Basic Statistics classes I'm taking in college.

Features:
- Mode
- Median
- Mean
- Standard Deviation
- Variance
- Range
- Coefficent of Variation
- Shape of Distribution (Symmetric, Rigth Skewed or Left Skewed)

You can use this in your browser or computer environment.
